# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to maintaining a community that is open, respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment. All community members, contributors, maintainers, and leaders are expected to participate in a manner that supports a safe and constructive environment.

Participation in the community must not be restricted or degraded on the basis of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual orientation and identity.

We expect all participants to act professionally, communicate respectfully, and contribute to an environment in which people can collaborate effectively.

## Community Standards

Community members are expected to demonstrate consideration and respect in their interactions with others. Appropriate conduct includes treating others with empathy and professionalism, respecting differing opinions and experiences, providing and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and prioritizing the health and interests of the broader community.

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances.
* Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, personal attacks, or political attacks directed at individuals.
* Harassment in public or private communication.
* Publishing or distributing another person's private or sensitive information without explicit permission.
* Intimidation, threatening behavior, or conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

These examples are illustrative and do not constitute an exhaustive definition of unacceptable conduct.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Community leaders and maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They are expected to apply the standards consistently, fairly, and proportionately.

Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or otherwise moderate comments, commits, code, documentation, wiki edits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Where appropriate, maintainers may provide an explanation for moderation decisions.

Community leaders may take corrective action when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, disruptive, or otherwise harmful to the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all official community spaces and activities.

It also applies when an individual is representing the Caracal community in a public setting. Examples include using an official email address, publishing through an official social media account, participating as an appointed representative at an event, or otherwise acting in an official capacity.

Community contributions are governed by the project's contribution process. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for information about contributing to the Apache-2.0 licensed codebase.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, threatening, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported privately to the community leaders responsible for enforcement:

**[support@caracal.run](mailto:support@caracal.run)**

Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Community leaders are expected to protect the privacy and security of individuals who report incidents. Information relating to a report will be handled confidentially to the extent reasonably possible and consistent with the requirements of the investigation and enforcement process.

## Enforcement

Enforcement actions are determined according to the severity, frequency, context, and impact of the reported behavior. Community leaders may apply the following measures individually or in combination.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:**
Behavior that is inappropriate, unprofessional, or unwelcome but does not require a more serious intervention.

**Possible consequence:**
A private written correction explaining the relevant standard and why the behavior was inappropriate. Where appropriate, the individual may be asked to acknowledge the impact of the behavior or provide an apology.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:**
A violation involving a single incident or a pattern of conduct that warrants a formal warning.

**Possible consequence:**
A formal warning describing the violation and the consequences of continued misconduct. Restrictions may be placed on interaction with affected individuals or participation in specific community spaces for a defined period.

Failure to comply with such restrictions may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community impact:**
A serious violation or sustained pattern of inappropriate conduct.

**Possible consequence:**
Temporary suspension from participation, communication, or public interaction within the community for a defined period.

During the suspension, the individual may be prohibited from contacting affected individuals or community leaders without prior authorization.

Further violations may result in a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community impact:**
A sustained or severe pattern of violations, including repeated harassment, serious misconduct, or aggressive or disparaging behavior directed toward individuals or protected groups.

**Possible consequence:**
Permanent removal from public participation and interaction within the community.
